About Movie Land

A simple guide to movies and series from around the world.

Our idea

Movie Land helps you find something to watch. We collect trending movies and popular series in one place, so you do not have to search many sites.

This website is a university project built with HTML, CSS and Bootstrap.

What you can do

Browse movies

See trending movies with their year, rating, director and genre.

Find series

Check popular shows, where they are available and when they started.

Send feedback

Tell us which movie or series we should add next.

How to use the site

  1. Open the Movies or Series page.
  2. Pick a title you like.
  3. Use the Contact page to suggest new titles.
